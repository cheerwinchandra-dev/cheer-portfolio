What I do

  • Machine Learning

    Predictive models in scikit-learn: logistic regression, random forests and gradient boosting, compared on PR-AUC, ROC-AUC and Brier score.

  • LLM Agents

    Agents on Claude, Gemini or local Ollama models that read text, rank priorities and draft messages, with every output validated.

  • Responsible AI

    Fairness audits across groups (false-positive and false-negative gaps), calibrated probabilities and plain-language explanations.

  • Data & Features

    Cleaning and shaping data with pandas and NumPy, engineering features and splitting by time so the evaluation stays honest.

  • Model Deployment

    Models served through FastAPI and shipped as live web and desktop apps, with drift checks after launch.

My process

  1. 01

    Frame

    Define the decision the model supports, the target and how success will be measured.

  2. 02

    Data

    Collect, clean and explore the data, and look for gaps and bias before training.

  3. 03

    Train

    Engineer features, start from a transparent baseline, then try stronger models.

  4. 04

    Evaluate

    Test on held-out data with imbalance-aware metrics, calibration and fairness by group.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Serve it behind an API, watch for drift and retrain when the data changes.

About me

I'm Cheerwin, an AI/ML engineer. I work across the whole machine-learning lifecycle: preparing the data, training and comparing models, calibrating and evaluating them, and serving them behind an API that people actually use.

I care about models people can trust. In EduGuard, sensitive attributes stay out of the model and are used only to audit it for gaps in false-positive and false-negative rates, and a person reviews every case. My LLM agents get de-identified prompts, validated outputs and a safe fallback.
